About

Ramona is a Certified Physician Assistant with over 25 years of experience in urgent, immediate, and family care across Northeastern Indiana. She's built a career on steady hands, quick judgment, and genuine care for patients — the same qualities that show up in her garden beds, her sewing room, and her kitchen.
